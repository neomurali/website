Commitment to Excellence

We ensure that our services are comparable to the best industry standards through regular audits, periodical training, and strict compliance with all Service Level Agreements (SLAs) and statutory obligations.

Service & Employee Welfare

We provide 24/7 security on a 12-hour shift basis with competitive tariffs. We strictly comply with all statutory obligations to ensure employee welfare:

  • EPF & Employees' State Insurance (ESI)
  • Proper uniforms, grooming, and training
  • Statutory bonuses and work norms
  • 100% compliance with all labor regulations

Quality Assurance & Billing

We maintain the highest industry standards through continuous evaluation and transparent operations:

  • Regular operational audits
  • Monthly evaluation reports from clients
  • Periodical training for SLA compliance
  • Bills submitted by the 1st of every month

Elite Recruitment Process

Our dedicated team of recruitment officers identifies individuals with devotion to the security profession:

  • Followed by rigorously vetted civilians
  • Comprehensive background verification
  • Detailed previous employment history checks

Comprehensive Training Methodology

Basic training is designated as per the PSA Act 2005-2008. Our extensive curriculum includes:

  • Physical drill, grooming, and crowd management

  • CCTV monitoring, access control & metal detectors
  • Monthly onsite training for client-specific procedures

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Phoenix Security Facility Management Services (PSFMS) provide?

PSFMS provides 24/7 corporate security, industrial security, residential security, comprehensive facility management (including housekeeping and maintenance), and skilled manpower deployment services tailored to corporate needs.

Is PSFMS a PSARA licensed and compliant security agency?

Yes, PSFMS is fully PSARA licensed and compliant. We strictly adhere to the PSA Act 2005-2008 and fulfill all statutory obligations including EPF, Employees' State Insurance (ESI), and statutory bonuses for our employees.

What is the recruitment and training process for PSFMS security personnel?

We give preference to Ex-Defence, Police, and Home Guards, followed by rigorously vetted civilians. Our training includes physical drill, crowd management, crisis response, fire fighting, first aid, CCTV monitoring, and monthly onsite training for client-specific procedures.

What are the billing terms for PSFMS security services?

As a service-oriented organization, bills are submitted to clients by the 1st of every succeeding month. We request payments to be processed within the 7th of the succeeding month to ensure smooth operations and employee welfare.
